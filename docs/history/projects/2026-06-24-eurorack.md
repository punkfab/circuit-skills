## Goal
Dan wanted a toolchain, not one app, for Eurorack modules: "have an idea and start simulating the circuit and have a buildable prototype for the entire cycle." The kickoff settled on "orchestrate + glue": ngspice for simulation, KiCad (`pcbnew` + `kicad-cli`) for PCB, silkscreen and Gerbers, and build123d for the panel and mounts. One `module.toml` per module is the shared spec, and a parts registry maps each part type to its footprint, pins, panel cutout and sim model. First slice: a passive attenuator. Second module: a VCO.

## Problems and fixes
1. **ngspice and PySpice not installed; no passwordless sudo.**
   - **Symptom:** `which ngspice` was empty, `import PySpice` gave a traceback, and sudo reported "sudo-rs: interactive authentication is required".
   - **Root cause:** a fresh machine.
   - **Tried:** the assistant could not apt-install.
   - **Resolution:** fixed. Dan installed `ngspice` 44.2 (`44.2+ds-1`). PySpice was never used; the sim leg shells out to `ngspice -b`.
   - **Landed:** README requirements table and the `make check` dependency probe.
2. **`pcbnew` not importable, then numpy shadowing.**
   - **Symptom:** `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'pcbnew'`. After `sys.path.insert(0,'/usr/lib/python3/dist-packages')`: `ImportError: Error importing numpy: you should not try to import numpy from its source directory`.
   - **Root cause:** KiCad's `pcbnew.py` and `_pcbnew.so` (a symlink to `_pcbnew.kiface`) live in system dist-packages, which is not on the pyenv 3.12.9 path. Inserting at the front made the system numpy shadow the pip numpy.
   - **Resolution:** fixed. *Append* rather than insert. Result: build123d 0.8.0, numpy 2.4.4 and pcbnew 9.0.3 in one interpreter.
   - **Landed:** `toolkit/_env.py` and the README.
3. **No usable Thonkiconn / PJ-320A jack footprint.**
   - **Symptom:** `LOAD FAIL Jack_3.5mm_PJ-320A_Horizontal`.
   - **Root cause:** that footprint is not in the KiCad 9 stock libraries.
   - **Resolution:** worked around. `Jack_3.5mm_CUI_SJ1-3523N_Horizontal` (pads T/S/R) is used as a stand-in.
   - **Status:** still open (README roadmap item "Real Thonkiconn footprints").
4. **First board: 28 DRC violations.**
   - **Symptom:** `kicad-cli pcb drc` reported copper_sliver 1, solder_mask_bridge 3, invalid_outline 4, copper_edge_clearance 2, silk_edge_clearance 14, tracks_crossing 1, shorting_items 1, hole_clearance 2.
   - **Root causes (three):**
     - The jack footprints carry their own Edge.Cuts slots, which made the outline malformed.
     - The naive straight star-tracks ran down pad columns and across NPTH holes.
     - Vendor silk overhung the 20 mm board.
   - **Resolution:** fixed through problems 5–10.
   - **Landed:** `toolkit/pcb.py`, commit `11e70a9`.
5. **pcbnew API name mismatch.**
   - **Symptom:** `AttributeError: 'BOARD_DESIGN_SETTINGS' object has no attribute 'SetCopperEdgeClearance'`.
   - **Root cause:** in 9.0.3 it is a member, not a setter.
   - **Resolution:** fixed with `settings.m_CopperEdgeClearance = …`.
   - **Landed:** `pcb.py`.
6. **Footprint fields come back as raw `SwigPyObject`.**
   - **Symptom:** `fp.Reference()` returned an unwrapped pointer, then `AttributeError: 'SwigPyObject' object has no attribute 'SetVisible'`.
   - **Root cause:** after `board.Add(fp)`, and after any `fp.Remove()`, SWIG hands back unwrapped pointers.
   - **Tried:** `fp.GetFields()` (works standalone) and calling it after Add (fails).
   - **Resolution:** fixed. Hide fields via `GetFields()` *before* `board.Add` and *before* any graphics removal.
   - **Landed:** `pcb.py`, with a comment.
7. **Segfault and later `FootprintLoad` failures.**
   - **Symptom:** exit code 139 (segfault) with an empty stdout, then `FootprintLoad` failing "deterministically even on the first call".
   - **Root cause:** `fp.Remove()` on footprint graphics corrupts the global footprint-plugin state, which breaks the *next* `FootprintLoad`. Bisected: `FootprintLoad` alone works.
   - **Resolution:** fixed. Never `Remove()`; move footprint Edge.Cuts and silk to `Dwgs.User` instead.
   - **Landed:** `_strip_footprint_graphics` in `pcb.py` and the commit message of `11e70a9`.
8. **SWIG leak spam.**
   - **Symptom:** hundreds of `swig/python detected a memory leak of type 'PCB_SHAPE *', no destructor found.` lines.
   - **Root cause:** SWIG binding noise.
   - **Resolution:** worked around by grepping it out (`grep -iv "deprecat\|warn\|leak"`). Never fixed.
9. **GND pour would not clear NPTH holes.**
   - **Symptom:** `DRC failed: 10 error(s) {'hole_clearance': 10}`, i.e. "board setup constraints hole clearance 0.2500 mm; actual 0.0000 mm" between `NPTH pad of J2` and `Zone [GND] on B.Cu`.
   - **Root cause:** `NewBoard()` defaults every clearance to 0, *and* the scripted zone filler does not knock out bare NPTH holes even with clearance set.
   - **Tried:**
     - `zone.SetLocalClearance(0.3 mm)`: no change.
     - `m_HoleClearance` / `m_MinClearance`: reloaded as 0.3 mm, still 10 errors.
     - `ds.GetDefault()`: does not exist.
   - **Resolution:** at the time, worked around by dropping the pour and maze-routing GND as tracks. **Reversed later** (problem 17): circular rule-area keepouts around each NPTH finally let the filler clear the holes.
   - **Landed:** `pcb.py` (`_add_npth_keepouts`), commit `90db98e`.
10. **Router traces too close together.**
    - **Symptom:** `{'clearance': 5}`, e.g. "board minimum clearance 0.2500 mm; actual 0.1657 mm" track-to-track and 0.0236 mm GND track-to-pad.
    - **Root cause:** the obstacle-inflation radius omitted the routed track's own half-width and had no grid-discretisation margin.
    - **Resolution:** fixed. Pads are inflated by `CLEARANCE + TRACK_WIDTH/2 + GRID` and tracks by `TRACK_WIDTH + CLEARANCE + GRID`, giving **DRC 0 errors, 0 warnings**.
    - **Landed:** `pcb.py`, commit `11e70a9`.
11. **`make drc` left a stray report and never failed.**
    - **Symptom:** `attenuator-drc.rpt` appeared in the repo root.
    - **Root cause:** `kicad-cli pcb drc` writes to the cwd by default and exits 0 on violations.
    - **Resolution:** fixed with `--output $(OUT)/pcb/$(MODULE)-drc.rpt --exit-code-violations`.
    - **Landed:** `Makefile`, commit `c703537`.
12. **VCO proof-of-concept oscillated at the wrong amplitude and frequency.**
    - **Symptom:** the scratch `vco.cir` gave TRI ±4.67 V (thresholds predict ±1.05 V), SQ ±10.5 V, 276.6 Hz.
    - **Root cause:** the behavioral op-amp's 100 Hz dominant pole was far too slow, so comparator delay, not OTA current, set the timing.
    - **Resolution:** fixed. Moved the pole to µs (`Cp n1 0 1.59n`). Result: TRI ±1.05 V, f = 93.90·CV + 1004.05 Hz, R² = 0.99990 over −4…+8 V.
    - **Landed:** scratch deck, then `toolkit/models.spice`.
13. **LM13700 subckt modelling.**
    - **Symptom:** the buffer source sign was wrong, and unused OTA/buffer pins floated.
    - **Resolution:** fixed. Corrected the B-source buffers, and the netlister now ties unconnected IC pins to 0.
    - **Landed:** `toolkit/models.spice` and `sim.py`, commit `ea7111f`.
14. **Horizontal axial resistors too wide for the dense board.**
    - **Symptom:** horizontal footprints are about 10 mm wide.
    - **Resolution:** fixed. Switched to `R_Axial_DIN0207_L6.3mm_D2.5mm_P2.54mm_Vertical` (courtyard 5.2×3.1 mm).
    - **Landed:** `parts.py`.
15. **Single-layer maze router cannot route the VCO.**
    - **Symptom:** `router failed to connect nets: ['SQ', 'SQ', 'SQ', 'CMPP', 'OTAIN', 'IABC', …]`.
    - **Root cause:** 16 parts, 13 nets, 2 ICs on one layer; nets must cross.
    - **Resolution:** superseded by the 2-layer router (problem 16) and then freerouting (problem 18).
16. **2-layer router bugs and congestion.**
    - **Symptom:** `IndexError: list index out of range` in `block_circle`. After fixing that, `failed: ['IABC','VCC','TRI','SQ']`. With high-fanout-first ordering, `['VCC','TRI','SQ']`. After a 12HP re-placement, `['VCC','TRI','SQ','CMPP','TRIOUT']`.
    - **Root cause:**
      - The pcbnew layer *id* (B_Cu = 31) was stored where a 0/1 index was expected.
      - The greedy route-once router with hand placement cannot converge on a dense board. Widening the board and packing resistors at 3 mm pitch made congestion worse.

## Decisions and reversals
- **Orchestrate + glue** (KiCad, ngspice, build123d) rather than building engines. Dan reframed it as a heterogeneous toolchain sharing `module.toml`.
- **Simulate the VCO core standalone before touching the toolkit.** This caught the slow op-amp model (problem 12) cheaply.
- **GND pour → routed GND tracks → pour again.** Dropped when the filler would not clear NPTH; restored with copper-pour-only keepouts.
- **Dan chose "Rip-up & reroute router"; the assistant shipped freerouting instead**, reasoning that "using a proven industrial router beats hand-rolling rip-up". The built-in maze router remains the `router = "maze"` default for simple boards. This is a reversal of Dan's explicit pick; the transcript does not show him objecting.
- **Placement is ~90% of routability.** Stated in `docs/AUTOROUTING.md`: "We never touched the router itself — every failure was placement or board-setup." This is the earliest statement of what became the `pcb-layout` skill's north star (inferred lineage).
